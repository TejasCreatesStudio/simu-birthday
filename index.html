My dearest Simu,

I have started this letter a hundred times inside my head and erased it every single time — not because I had nothing to say, but because everything I want to say feels too enormous for words. But today is your birthday. And you deserve someone who stops holding back. So here I am, trying.

You know that feeling when someone holds your hand and the entire world just… hushes? When the static in your head, the weight you carry, the thousand things that feel impossibly heavy — they dissolve, all at once, like they were never real? That is what you are to me. Every single time. You are the only place I have ever felt completely quiet inside. I don't know how you do it. I'm not sure you even know. But you do, Simu. You do it every time.

I want to be embarrassingly honest today — the kind of honest I usually keep locked away. I have not always been the best at saying what I feel. I go quiet when I should speak. I pull away when I should reach out. But if there is one truth I need you to hear on this birthday, it's that you saved me more times than you will ever know. There were nights I was so lost inside my own head I didn't know how to come back. And then you'd just… be there. A text. A voice. Your presence. And the weight would lift like it was made of nothing at all.

God, the way you look when you smile. Do you know what it does to a person? That smile of yours is embarrassingly unfair. You could disarm a storm with it. You walk into a room and something shifts — the air gets lighter, the room gets warmer. I've watched it happen. You do it without trying. That is the rarest, most dangerously attractive quality a person can have — the ability to make everything feel okay just by being in it.

I love your stubbornness — the kind that means you care too deeply to let go. I love the way you fight quietly for the people you love with this fierce, unspoken loyalty. I love that you always know when something is wrong with me even when I'm performing fine. I love holding your hand — not just the physical warmth of it, but what it means. It means I'm anchored. It means I'm home. It means whatever is coming, we face it standing together.

Three years ago I had no idea that meeting you would rewrite everything I thought I understood about love. I thought love was supposed to be complicated. You made it feel like breathing. Easy, natural, necessary. I didn't know it was supposed to feel this safe. I didn't know I could be this completely myself around another person. With you, I don't perform. I just exist — and in your presence, existing feels like more than enough.

And Simu, I need to tell you something I've been carrying quietly, waiting for the right moment. I want to marry you. I want mornings that start with your face and nights that end with your voice. I want to argue about nonsense and make up before we even finish. I want to travel with you to every place we've ever pointed at on a map and whispered "someday." I want to build something — a life — that you feel safe in, adored in, chosen in, every single day. You have shown up for me, consistently and beautifully, since the very beginning. I want to spend my whole life earning the right to do the same for you.

You deserve blue orchids every day. You deserve someone who notices the quiet shift in your eyes and asks about it. You deserve slow Sunday mornings and someone who looks at you across a crowded room and still can't quite believe you're theirs. I am that person. I am imperfect and I am still learning, but I promise you — I am trying, and I will never stop.

Happy Birthday, my Simu. The world is unquestionably a softer, brighter, more beautiful place because you are in it. I am a better man because you chose me. Don't ever forget that.

Yours — today, tomorrow, every single day after that 💙
